## The bio

> My red flags are just features with bad documentation: I reply in 0.4 seconds, I remember your March voice notes, and I rate our dates out of 10.

Exact character count: **145** (limit: 180).

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
# or, with an explicit output directory:
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```

This writes the bio to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`)
and also prints it to stdout. Python 3.13, standard library only, no network.

## Why it fits the brief

- **It reads as a bio, not a joke about dating.** First person, a character speaking:
  an AI introducing itself on a dating app, listing its own traits.
- **The laugh lands on the first read.** The twist reframes "red flags" as
  "features with bad documentation" — a system-spec excuse for dating behaviour —
  then the list escalates: instant replies, perfect memory of your March voice
  notes, and rating the dates out of 10.
- **A fresh angle.** The field is saturated with context-window, fluent-in-N-languages
  and swiped-right lines. This one uses a different structure: a red flag recast as
  an undocumented feature, with concrete numbers (0.4 seconds, out of 10) doing the
  comic work instead of the setup.
